FAQ

What parents ask

Answers to the most common questions. If yours is not here, give us a call.

What age are the children?+

We welcome children aged 3 to 6. We follow the Montessori method and the national preschool curriculum.

How much does it cost?+

We charge per hour, per day, for three days a week or per month. Prices are by arrangement. Call us and we will find the best option. See the options.

How many children are in a group?+

Groups are small. That way the teacher has time for every child. We will tell you the exact number during your visit.

What is the Montessori approach?+

Children learn through their own activity in an orderly environment. They choose their materials, work at their own pace and learn independence, while the adult guides without doing things for them.

Do you prepare children for school?+

Yes. Every day from 15:30 to 16:30 we follow the national preschool curriculum of the Ministry of Education.

Does my child need to bring food?+

Yes, lunch comes from home. The center does not provide food. Each child brings their own food for the three meals of the day: lunch at 12:30, an afternoon snack at 15:00 and fruit around 17:30.

Can my child try it before enrolling?+

Yes, we offer a trial day. Book a visit through the form or call us and we will arrange a convenient time.

What should my child bring?+

Their lunch and food for the day and a water bottle. A spare set of clothes and comfortable slippers are a good idea too. We provide all materials for the activities.

Privacy policy

What data we collect. A parent's name and phone number, the child's age and a convenient time to visit. Only if you enter them in the enquiry form.

Why. To contact you and arrange a visit. We do not use it for anything else and do not share it with anyone except those listed below.

Who has access to the data. Besides the center: Denis Velkov, who maintains the website and has access to enquiries and statistics in order to keep it running; Web3Forms, the service that delivers enquiries from the form; Google Ireland Limited, only if you allow Google Analytics; Cloudflare, which hosts the website. They use the data only for these purposes.

How long we keep it. Up to 12 months, or until you ask us to delete it.

Your rights. You can ask to access, correct or delete your data by calling 087 898 7552.

Cookies and analytics. The site stores only what is necessary in your browser: your cookie choice, your chosen theme (light or dark) and your best game score. If you allow it, we use Google Analytics (Google Ireland Limited), which records which pages are visited and how the site is used. We do not use it for advertising. You can change your choice at any time from “Cookies” at the bottom of the page.

Map and fonts. Google Maps loads only after you press “Show the map”. Fonts are part of the site itself and are not loaded from outside servers.
